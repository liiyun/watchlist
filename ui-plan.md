# Marginalia — Complete Product & Visual Design Brief

## Project Overview

**Marginalia** is a reading, annotation, and intellectual reflection app designed for thoughtful readers who want to preserve the lessons, ideas, quotes, and insights they encounter through books.

The app is inspired by the literary tradition of “marginalia” — notes written in the margins of books.

Marginalia is not simply a reading tracker.
It is:

* a personal intellectual archive
* a commonplace book
* a reading journal
* a reflection system
* a lifelong companion for collecting wisdom

The emotional goal is to create an experience that feels calm, scholarly, editorial, tactile, reflective, and deeply personal.

The app should feel like:

> a quiet digital study room where ideas are collected slowly over time.

---

Marginalia exists somewhere between:
